    🧍‍♂️About-me

    😄😄 Hi! My name is Jinfu Liu. I am currently an algorithm engineer at DJI. My work focuses on developing On-device AI Technologies and integrating them into DJI’s product lines, including the Drone series and the OSMO series.
    Prior to that, I received my M.S. degree in Sun Yat-sen University (supervised by Prof.Mengyuan Liu), where my research are primarily focused on Video Understanding. I received my B.S. degree in Automation from Wuhan University of Technology.
    During my studies, I was also an intern at Tencent AMS, MeiTuan, and Tencent AI Lab, where I gained experience in business scenarios such as advertising, e-commerce, and gaming. My research involved LLMs-based content understanding, video generation, and digital human generation.
    Now my research interests are primarily focused on LLMs-based Content Understanding/Generation, MLLMs Agent Application, and On-device AI Technologies. I was also a reviewer in the aforementioned fields (e.g. in the IEEE-TPAMI, IEEE-TMM, IEEE-TCSVT, ECCV, ACM MM, and ICME).
